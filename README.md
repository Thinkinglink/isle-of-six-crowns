## Play together

Open **Friends & Party** from the main menu or the map (key **F**).

- **Add friends:** share your 8-digit friend code, or let them scan your QR code. You're added to each other's lists automatically.
- **Invite:** press **Invite** next to an online friend. Up to 4 players, on any mix of Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone and iPad.
- **Harder together:** mobs in every level and boss health are multiplied by the number of players (2 players = ×2, 4 players = ×4).
- The party leader picks the level. Everyone gets their own loot, and a fallen player gets back up next to a teammate.
- If a friend takes you to an island you haven't reached yet, you keep the loot, but it doesn't count as cleared for you. You still unlock islands in order on your own save.

## 🌊 The Outer Isles

Beat the Hollow Sovereign to save the isle, then press **→** at the bottom right of the map to sail to a second map:

- **Tempest Monument**: a storm-wracked island ruled by Thunderhead the Storm Sentinel.
- **Overgrown Temple**: jungle ruins guarded by the Mossback Behemoth.
- **Deep Harvest Sea**: an underwater reef where you have 30 seconds of air. Stand on the glowing air pods to breathe. Lanternmaw hunts in the deepest trench.

Each has 4 levels and a boss, with cutscenes and its own loot.

## 🕳️ The Deep Dark

Defeat Lanternmaw and a new **→** appears on the Outer Isles map. The Deep Dark is one enormous cavern with 6 levels, and the Echo Tyrant guards the great portal in the 7th. Beat it to open the portal and step through.

## 🌀 The Shift Dimension

On the other side of the portal are four lands, each with 4 levels and a boss in the 5th:

- **Sculk Obsession**: sculk-infected creatures and the Sculk Monstrosity.
- **Monarch Kingdom**: the Monarch's knights, hounds and mages, and the Monarch himself.
- **Grass of Harmony**: seedlings, sprouts, sentinels, nesters and pollinators, led by the Harmonizer.
- **Singer's Meadows**: sculk-infected and soul-corrupted versions of the meadow creatures, and the Corrupted Singer.

## 🔥 The Forge and 🌌 The Void

Once the great portal is open, the Deep Dark map has **three portals**: the Shift, the Forge and the Void. The first level of each is open straight away.

- **The Forge** (fire, lava and iron): Cinder Foundry (the Foundry Colossus), Brimstone Wastes (the Brimstone Wyrm), Iron Citadel (the Iron Warlord) and The Molten Throne (the Forge King).
- **The Void** (an endless starless dark): Starless Expanse (the Hollow Watcher), Shattered Rift (the Rift Weaver), Null Gardens (the Null Bloom) and The Endless Edge (the Endless One).

Each land has 4 levels and a boss, its own monsters and its own weapons, armor and artifacts. Every boss also drops its own summoning artifact. Click the portal on a dimension's map to go back to the Deep Dark. Every boss now arrives through a summoning circle in its own action cutscene.

## 🎃 Halloween event (until October 31)

Hollowshade Isle has risen off the coast. Fight through 7 haunted levels and face the Pumpkin King in the 8th. Anything you find there stays in your pack after the event.

## ⚔️ Difficulty

Press **Difficulty** at the top of the map to switch between **Easy** (monsters and bosses have a third less health, hit softer and move slower), **Medium** (normal) and **Hard** (they hit harder and move faster). In co-op, the party leader's setting is used.

## ✦ Enchanting

Select any weapon, armor or artifact in your Pack and press **✦ Enchant** (above the Equip button). It is free, and each item can be enchanted once. An enchanted item gets:

- **+25% power**, and its damage, health or strength goes up
- **a new enchantment** it didn't have yet
- **a soul ability**: Soul Strike for weapons, Soul Barrier for armor, Soul Echo for artifacts
- **a purple aura**, both in your pack and around your hero in the game

## 🐺 Summoning artifacts

50 new artifacts summon allies that fight beside you: wolves, golems, knights, skeleton archers, exploding mites, healing sprites and more. Every boss of the Outer Isles and the Shift Dimension now drops its own guardian summoning artifact as well as its normal one. Every summoned pet has its own health bar and stays with you until it is defeated (up to 10 at a time). Pets tear through regular monsters but only chip away at bosses.

## ✨ The Core of Creations

Conquer the Shift, the Forge **and** the Void, then press **Core of Creations →** on the Deep Dark map. One great island, five cores, each with 4 levels and a boss:

1. **Core of Knowledge**: the Ancient Tome, a giant living book that rains glowing runes on you.
2. **Core of Destruction**: the God of Destruction, who smashes straight through rocks and walls. Don't hide behind cover for long.
3. **Core of Immunity**: the Radiant Seraph, a six-winged god of light who raises walls of light around you and builds light towers that shoot.
4. **Core of Infection**: the Blight Mother, who squirts poison that leaves puddles. Standing in a puddle hurts and slows you.
5. **Core of Forms**: the Shapeless One, which fights in three forms: Stone, then Flame at 2/3 health, then Stars at 1/3 health, with a cutscene each time it changes.

Every core has its own monsters, weapons, armor, artifacts and a guardian summoning artifact.

## 🏠 Home menu rewards

- Reach the **Outer Isles** and the home menu shows a floating, broken, corrupted heart fragment.
- Reach the **Deep Dark** and the **Shift Dimension** and it changes to swirling souls.

## 🛒 Shop

Open it from the map with **Shop** (key **B**): weekly limited weapons, armor and artifacts, 9 permanent upgrades, and 125 skins from Common to Mythic.

## Updates

The game updates itself when you open it, on every device. Your progress is always kept.

## 🎬 Boss defeat cutscenes

Every boss now gets its own defeat cutscene: its last words, its collapse, souls rising out of the arena, and a final word from you.

## 👹 Boss overhaul

Every boss is now built from far finer, smoother geometry, with dark corruption veins that pulse in its own colour. Each one carries an immense aura: a roaring flame shell, a spinning rune circle, glowing ground cracks, shock rings, a vortex of motes, crackling arcs and floating rubble. The aura flares when the boss attacks and is toned to the arena so the boss always stays visible. Bosses also breathe, turn smoothly, track you with their heads and kick up dust as they walk.

The Sculk Monstrosity and the Corrupted Singer have their own story cutscenes: the Singer gathers lost souls into a soul block, the sculk spins a cocoon around it and the Monstrosity hatches; later the soul block is forced into the Singer itself, and defeating it heals it.

## ⚔️ Signature moves, unique cutscenes, themed levels

- **Every boss has its own signature move**, called out on screen when it's used: Thornwood Prison, Living Sandstorm, Avalanche, Thunder Chase, Earthshaker Leap, Deadly Lure, Royal Execution, Hammerfall, Watcher's Gaze, Storm of Pages, World Splitter, Judgment of Light, Requiem and many more.
- **Every boss arrives its own way** (bursting from the ground, crashing down from the sky, charging in, flickering in, assembling from light, waking from stone…) and shows off its signature move in its intro.
- **Every boss dies its own way**: shattering, melting, burning to ash, sinking, exploding, dissolving into light or toppling.
- **Every level is dressed after its title**: halls get tiled floors and pillars, libraries get bookshelves, forges get lava cracks and anvils, gardens get flowers, royal levels get carpets and banners, caverns are darker and twistier, plains are wide open, and more. Layouts change too: halls, caves, long roads or open plains.
- Monsters, terrain and scenery are built from much finer geometry.

## 👾 168 unique monsters

Every monster in every region now has its own design: 17 different body types (walkers, hulking brutes, golems, four-legged beasts, insects, serpents, crabs, mantises, birds, walking plants, totems, slimes, jellies, floating mages, flame wisps, tripods and walking bombs) combined with their own heads, backs, weapons and tails. No two of the 168 monsters share a design, and each one is shaped to fit its name.

## 🎵 Music and sound

Every level and every boss has its own looping music, composed live by the game, so no two places sound the same (162 different tracks). The main menu has a heroic theme. Every swing, arrow, roll, potion, artifact, hit, pickup, chest and monster has a sound, and every boss has its own roar and attack sounds. Turn music and effects up or down with the Sound button on the main menu or in the pause menu.

## ⚔️ Equip best

The Equip best button in your pack puts on your strongest weapon, armor and artifacts in one tap. Bosses now have five times as much health.

## 🗝️ Bigger levels, locked gates and key golems

Levels are now about twice as large, with more rooms and more monsters. Each level's exit is sealed behind an iron gate. Find the glowing key golem, lead it to the gate and the gate opens; bigger levels have two gates and two key golems. Every region of the first island now has five levels before its guardian, and your old progress carries over.

## 🎸 A real band for every track, and redesigned bosses

The music now uses real-sounding instruments in place of thin beeps: piano, strings, choir, brass, French horn, flute, fiddle, tin whistle, harp, electric guitar, electric piano, saxophone, pipe organ, marimba, kalimba and bells, over rock, metal, orchestral, taiko, marching, folk, jazz brush and electronic drum kits. Every track is played in its own style (rock, metal, orchestral, epic choir, piano, folk, jazz, ambient, tribal, march, synthwave or cathedral organ), with an intro, a main theme, a contrasting part and a big finish. Every melody stays in its instrument's comfortable range, so nothing squeaks.

Eighteen bosses have been rebuilt from scratch, with sculpted bodies, horns, claws, armour, cloth and glowing details. Each keeps its theme but is bigger and scarier: the Bramble King, Sand Sultan Ashkar, Mother Murk, the Rime Warden, the Cinderheart Colossus, the Pumpkin King, the Hollow Sovereign and the Sovereign Unbound, Thunderhead, the Mossback Behemoth, Lanternmaw, the Echo Tyrant, the Monarch, the Harmonizer, the Corrupted Singer, the Foundry Colossus, the Brimstone Wyrm and the Iron Warlord. The rest follow in the next update.
